# FAQ

**What is an audience response system?**
A system that collects votes or answers from a group in real time using keypads or smartphones, a receiver and software. → [Guide](guides/audience-response-systems-guide.md)

**What is the difference between a keypad and a clicker?**
None: both terms describe the same voting device.

**Do keypads need internet or Wi-Fi?**
No. They transmit by radio to a receiver on the PC. Only browser voting (BYOPAD™) needs internet.

**Keypads or smartphones?**
Keypads for critical votes, weak networks and focus; smartphones for remote participants; hybrid for both. → [Comparison](guides/keypads-vs-smartphones.md)

**Which keypad should I choose?**
Interact Mini (5 buttons) for councils and classrooms, Interact (10) for versatile use, Interact Pro (rechargeable, about 100 m) for large AGMs and elections, Mini+ Gen. 2 for very large systems and competitions. → [Overview](products/reply-keypads-overview.md)

**Can votes be weighted and proxies handled?**
Yes, with BoardARS™ and 4elections™. → [Board meetings and AGMs](solutions/board-meetings-and-agms.md)

**Are secret ballots possible?**
Yes; only totals are stored for secret votes. Whether a procedure is legally valid depends on your statutes and applicable law.

**Can I combine on-site and remote voting?**
Yes, with keypads plus BYOPAD™. → [Hybrid voting](solutions/hybrid-voting-byopad.md)

**Can I rent keypads?**
Yes: <https://www.replysystems.com/rent-keypads/>. Purchase: <https://www.replysystems.com/buy-a-voting-system/>.

**Are there recurring licence fees?**
Software licences are one-off purchases and do not expire. Updates and support are included in the first year; renewal of the maintenance programme is optional.

**Is it legally compliant?**
The software produces traceable results and reports. Whether a voting method is permitted is governed by law, statutes and rules of procedure. This is not legal advice.
