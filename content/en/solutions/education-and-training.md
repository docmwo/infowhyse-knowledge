# EdiVote® 3.0 for training, classrooms and conferences

## Short answer

**EdiVote® 3.0** is a PowerPoint add-in that turns slides into live votes, quizzes and polls. Participants answer with **Reply® keypads** or **smartphones (BYOPAD™)**, and results appear in the presentation immediately. It works with Microsoft PowerPoint for Windows from version 2016 up to Office 365 (not the browser-only version and not Office for Mac).

## Features

- Voting types: participant answers, multiple choice, ranking, ratings, free numeric entry
- Quizzes for individuals or teams
- Participant groups and demographics
- Advanced Microsoft Excel reports
- Full PowerPoint integration; a demo (authoring) mode supports up to 5 keypads
- Compatible with all Reply® Interact keypads and BYOPAD™ 3.0

## Typical uses

- **Classrooms and universities:** quizzes, learning games, quick class polls; reports show what students know and what needs revision
- **Corporate training, coaching and consulting:** live polls, knowledge checks, anonymous feedback, strategy workshops
- **Conferences and medical education (CME):** audience questions, knowledge checks, opinion polls
- **Decision support at meetings and shareholder resolutions**

## Why keypads in training?

No distraction from email or messengers, no dependence on internet or Wi-Fi, instant and precise results, simple to use.

## Recommended keypads

[Reply® Interact Mini](../products/reply-keypads-overview.md) (up to 5 answers, ideal for classrooms) or Interact (up to 10 answers).
