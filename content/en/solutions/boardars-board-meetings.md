# BoardARS™ for board meetings, member meetings and associations

## Short answer

**BoardARS™** is Infowhyse's voting software for **simple member meetings, board meetings and associations**. It is built for straightforward votes and single elections of up to 5 people, records voting behaviour, and works **offline** (Reply® Interact Mini), **online** via browser (BYOPAD™) or **hybrid**. For general meetings, AGMs and complex elections see [4elections™](4elections-agms.md).

## Features

- Open and secret ballots (secret ballots store totals only)
- Voting behaviour output for open votes
- Voting rights administration and proxy transfer
- Live attendance tracking, editing and visualisation
- Head-count and weighted votes (one weighting) side by side
- Vote counting including abstentions
- Single election of up to 5 people
- Requests to speak and motions on the keypad; proposals typed in or imported
- Excel report per resolution, optional screenshot
- Modes: offline (keypads), online (browser), hybrid

## Limits

For several different weightings, complex election modes, free quorums or elections of more than 5 people, use [4elections™](4elections-agms.md) (offline only).
