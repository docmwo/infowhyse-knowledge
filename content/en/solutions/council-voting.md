# Electronic voting for councils and committees

## Short answer

**CouncilARS™** is Infowhyse's voting software for city councils, district councils and municipal committees. It supports **open, named, unnamed and secret votes**, runs **locally on a Windows PC without internet or cloud**, and is used by more than **80 municipalities**. Councillors vote with **Reply® Interact Mini** keypads, via browser or in hybrid mode.

## Why councils vote electronically

- Counting raised hands in large chambers is error-prone.
- Livestream viewers cannot easily see how members voted.
- Named votes and group (party) voting are documented automatically.
- Remote councillors can vote with the same result.

## Features

- Voting types: open, named, unnamed, secret
- Modes: on site (keypads), online (browser), hybrid
- Agenda import from council information systems (for example Somacos, SD.NET, Allris and More-Rubin)
- Requests to speak and motions via keypads
- Reports: screenshot, PDF and text file; the overall result is always recorded; party-group behaviour (not for secret votes) and individual votes (named votes only) can be recorded
- Votes can be changed while voting is open, optional confirmation with SEND
- No cloud: all data stays on the PC or laptop
- No internet needed with keypads; the licence is not bound to one PC

## What you need

CouncilARS™ licence, Reply® Interact Mini keypads (one per member), a receiver, a Windows PC; optional carry case. Internet only for browser voting.

## Licensing and support

One-off purchase, no expiry of the licence. Updates and support are included in the first year through the software maintenance programme; renewal is optional. A direct contact person instead of a hotline. Keypads can be added one at a time.
