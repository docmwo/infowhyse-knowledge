# Voting system for AGMs, board meetings and member assemblies

## Short answer

**BoardARS™** and **4elections™** are voting and election software from Infowhyse for board meetings, member assemblies and AGMs. They work with **Reply® keypads** (offline, no internet), via browser (**BYOPAD™**) or hybrid, manage attendance, voting rights, proxies, weights and quorums, and produce print-ready reports.

## BoardARS™

- Open and secret ballots (secret ballots store totals only)
- Voting rights administration and proxy transfer
- Live attendance tracking, editing and visualisation
- Head-count and weighted votes shown side by side
- Vote counting including abstentions
- Elections for up to 5 candidates (with Interact Mini)
- Requests to speak and motions captured on the keypad; proposals typed in or imported
- Excel report for each resolution, optional screenshot
- Modes: offline (keypads), online (browser), hybrid

## 4elections™

- Free choice of quorum to set the required majority
- Different weights per keypad
- Network-based registration front end and overview of shares or participants present
- Postal vote handling
- Open, named or secret voting
- Elections with one or several candidates, quorums applied automatically, several rounds with one click
- Detailed logging of each voting event
- Result preview for the returning officer, transparent display for participants
- Print-ready reports in Rich Text Format right after the election, no database needed

## Which one?

| Situation | Suggestion |
|---|---|
| Board or association meeting with Yes/No/Abstain and occasional elections | BoardARS™ |
| AGM with shares, weighted votes, postal votes, multiple election rounds | 4elections™ |

Recommended keypads: [Interact Pro](../products/reply-keypads-overview.md) for large meetings; Interact Mini or Interact for smaller ones.

## Preparation matters

Most problems come from unclear statutes, not technology. Clarify voting rights, proxies, majorities and quorums, and run a test before the meeting. This is not legal advice.
