# Hybrid voting and smartphone voting with Reply® BYOPAD™ 3.0

## Short answer

**Reply® BYOPAD™ 3.0** ("Bring Your Own Pad") is a browser-based voting tool: participants vote on smartphone, tablet or laptop **without an app, download or installation**. It works on site, remotely or **hybrid** together with Reply® keypads, so on-site and remote participants vote into **one result**.

## How it works

1. The presenter starts a BYOPAD session in EdiVote® and sets an **event code**.
2. Participants open the voting page (`byopad.online`) and enter the code, or scan a QR code.
3. Questions appear on their device; votes are captured and displayed instantly.

Internet access is required for presenter and participants.

## Session types and modes

- Anonymous, anonymous with session password, or identified login with individual passwords (CSV list)
- Virtual keypad mode (full keypad or Yes/No/Abstain) or live mode showing question and answers on the phone

## Works with

EdiVote® Pro 3.0 (full feature set), and in hybrid mode with Reply® Interact Mini, Interact, Interact Pro, Mini+ and other Reply® keypads. BYOPAD™ can also be used with CouncilARS™ and BoardARS™, though not all EdiVote® features apply there.

## Data protection

No user registration, no unnecessary personal data. Browser votes pass through a server, and session data is deleted after the session. Results and reports remain on the organiser's PC. Use hard-to-guess event codes and passwords for sensitive sessions.
