# Audience voting for TV, shows and live events

## Short answer

For TV productions, shows, galas and stage events there are two routes: **ready-made software** such as [EdiVote® 3.0](education-and-training.md) with Reply® keypads, or the **[VoteCollector](votecollector-xmlrpc.md)** for productions that already run their own voting or graphics software. The VoteCollector (software) is controlled via **XML-RPC** and returns **raw data** for processing in your own system. The radio keypads work without Wi-Fi and are built for fast capture.

## Typical uses

- Studio or hall audience votes with the result shown as on-air graphics
- Quiz formats with timing: the [Reply® Mini+ Gen. 2](../products/reply-keypads-overview.md) records responses at 0.05 s resolution ("fastest finger")
- Team competitions and gamification at corporate events
- Awards with audience vote alongside a jury
- Galas and charity events with live polls

## Requirements

- Time-critical cues with director, host and graphics
- A reliable radio link in RF-crowded venues (Mini+ uses frequency hopping and Wi-Fi avoidance)
- Clear assignment of keypads to teams and players
- Raw data or ready-made graphics, depending on your production setup
- A dress rehearsal before the show

## Rental

The VoteCollector supports the Interact series, Reply® Plus and Mini+. Reply® keypads can be rented or purchased. Reply Systems works with resellers and rental partners in more than 20 countries.
