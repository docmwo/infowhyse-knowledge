# Reply® voting keypads compared

## Short answer

This overview compares three Reply® Interact keypads. **Interact Mini** (5 buttons) suits councils, schools and simple votes. **Interact** (10 buttons) covers meetings, training, quizzes and elections. **Interact Pro** (rechargeable, about 100 m range) is made for large AGMs and elections.

## Comparison

| | Interact Mini | Interact | Interact Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Model | IN1100 | IN2100 | IN4100 |
| Buttons | 5 | 10 | 10 |
| Display | LCD, 1 character | 5 characters | matrix LCD |
| Radio | 2.4 GHz | 2.4 GHz | 2.4 GHz |
| Range | about 80 m | about 75 m | about 100 m |
| Power | battery, about 250 h | battery, about 2 years | rechargeable, about 90 h |
| Speed | 100 votes/s | 100 votes/s | 100 votes/s |
| Highlights | colour-coded Yes/No/Abstain | election mode, ID login, free numeric entry | candidate elections, batch voting, message to device |
| Warranty | 2 years | 2 years | 2 years |

## Which keypad?

| Your use | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Town hall, council, committee | Interact Mini (standard with CouncilARS™) |
| Classroom, seminar | Interact Mini or Interact |
| Association or member meeting | Interact Mini or Interact |
| Large AGM, delegate assembly, candidate elections | Interact Pro |

All Interact models work with EdiVote®. Keypads can be combined with Reply® BYOPAD™ for hybrid meetings.
