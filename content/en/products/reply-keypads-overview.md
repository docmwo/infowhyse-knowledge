# Reply® voting keypads compared

## Short answer

Four Reply® keypads are currently available. **Interact Mini** (5 buttons) suits councils, schools and simple votes. **Interact** (10 buttons) covers meetings, training, quizzes and elections. **Interact Pro** (rechargeable, about 100 m range) is made for large AGMs and elections. **Mini+ Gen. 2** (14 buttons) serves very large systems and competitions.

## Comparison

| | Interact Mini | Interact | Interact Pro | Mini+ Gen. 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Model | IN1100 | IN2100 | IN4100 | WRS7100 |
| Buttons | 5 | 10 | 10 | 14 |
| Display | LCD, 1 character | 5 characters | matrix LCD | LED confirmation |
| Radio | 2.4 GHz | 2.4 GHz | 2.4 GHz | 2.4 GHz FHSS |
| Range | about 80 m | about 75 m | about 100 m | indoor, about 90 × 90 m |
| Power | battery, about 250 h | battery, about 2 years | rechargeable, about 90 h | coin cell, about 25,000 responses |
| Speed | 100 votes/s | 100 votes/s | 100 votes/s | 200 keypads/s |
| Highlights | colour-coded Yes/No/Abstain | election mode, ID login, free numeric entry | candidate elections, batch voting, message to device | speed scoring 50 ms, up to 15,500 per room |
| Warranty | 2 years | 2 years | 2 years | 2 years |

## Which keypad?

| Your use | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Town hall, council, committee | Interact Mini (standard with CouncilARS™) |
| Classroom, seminar | Interact Mini or Interact |
| Association or member meeting | Interact Mini or Interact |
| Large AGM, delegate assembly, candidate elections | Interact Pro |
| Very large systems, many answer options, fastest-finger quiz | Mini+ Gen. 2 |

All Interact models work with EdiVote® 3.0. Keypads can be combined with Reply® BYOPAD™ for hybrid meetings. Mini+ figures are taken from the manufacturer datasheet.
