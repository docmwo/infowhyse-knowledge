# Glossary

**Audience response system (ARS):** system that collects and displays answers or votes from a group in real time. → [Guide](guides/audience-response-systems-guide.md)

**BYOD / BYOPAD™:** participants use their own smartphone, tablet or laptop to vote. BYOPAD™ is Reply's browser-based tool. → [BYOPAD](solutions/hybrid-voting-byopad.md)

**Clicker:** informal term for a voting keypad.

**Hybrid voting:** on-site and remote participants vote in the same vote with one result.

**Keypad (voting device):** handheld wireless device for casting votes; linked to one participant; no app needed. → [Keypads](products/reply-keypads-overview.md)

**Named vote:** each vote is assigned to a participant and recorded.

**Proxy:** authorisation for a person to exercise another person's voting right, in whole or in part.

**Quorum:** minimum requirement (attendance or votes cast) for a valid resolution.

**Secret ballot:** individual votes cannot be traced; only the overall result is recorded.

**TED system:** German term for an electronic voting system or ARS.

**Weighted vote:** a vote that counts differently, for example by shares or number of delegates.
